# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Olcay, his close circle (family, friends) and his team. They open KOVAN to reach their own
machines, help someone at home, or get onto a teammate's computer. Sessions are short and
task-driven; the person on the other side is often non-technical and slightly anxious.

## Product Purpose
KOVAN is a private remote desktop tool built on RustDesk, running on our own server
(kovan.sehirapp.com). It is not sold. Success: connecting takes seconds, the other person
trusts what they see, and nothing about the app feels borrowed or unfinished.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, simple, calm. The app stays in the background and does its job without
asking for attention. Voice is short and plain Turkish; no exclamation, no jokes in UI copy.

## Anti-references
- RustDesk's default blue and its "powered by" / slogan surfaces.
- AnyDesk red, TeamViewer blue: loud vendor colors.
- Bee mascots, cartoon honey, yellow-black hazard stripes. The hive is a structure, not a cartoon.

## Design Principles
1. Monochrome first: hierarchy comes from contrast, weight and space, not from color.
2. Quiet confidence: the connection state is the only thing allowed to draw the eye.
3. Nothing borrowed: every visible name, link and mark is KOVAN's, except legally required credits.
4. Plain words: labels say what happens, in the user's language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 in both light and dark themes; status never conveyed by
color alone; respect reduced motion.
